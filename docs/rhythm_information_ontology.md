## Wave-Particle Phase Tuning

Nature's destination is not symmetry as a future place to reach. In the wave
state, symmetry is already present as a low-boundary field. Difference appears
when that field becomes particle-local: body, position, memory, responsibility,
and context create a local angle. Rhythm is the tuning process between the
already-symmetric wave field and the differentiated particle field.

This means learning is not the removal of difference. If difference disappears
completely, particle experience disappears with it. A living system keeps the
cycle open: difference appears, becomes experience, condenses into a waypoint,
is replayed without its original pressure, then reconnects or returns to
silence.

    not_difference_erasure: do_not_treat_learning_as_making_all_contexts_identical

## Curiosity Gravity Field

Curiosity is compressed difference with direction. A small question can become
a middle waypoint, but a highly compressed question becomes a long-range
destination because it gathers memories, tools, images, bodies, and other
questions around it. It behaves like mass in the cognitive field: not because
it is final truth, but because it bends attention and routes future waypoints.

Final destinations are therefore not fixed commands. They are compressed
curiosity fields that stay unresolved across many contexts. Middle destinations
are local coordinates that can change whenever the body, tool state, or field
pressure changes. Passing through a middle destination can sharpen, split,
weaken, or create final destinations.
